## 1. Key Engineering, Kernel & Roofline Discoveries

1. **Large-Microbatch `mb=64..72` + `HybridAC N=12..16` + `SBDP` + `PureFusedOpt` (`v5p_p9_02` -> `25.76%` MFU on `v5p-16`, `24.88%` MFU on `v5p-32`; `v7x_p12_04` -> `18.58%` MFU on `v7x-16`):**
   - Increasing per-rank tokens (`mb=48 -> mb=64` = `131,072` tok/rank = `3,072` tokens/expert across 256 top-6 experts, and `mb=72` = `3,456` tokens/expert) boosts GroupedMM MXU tile occupancy while amortizing fixed `79.6 GB` `BF16` parameter `AllGather` + `ReduceScatter` communication by `1.33×–1.50×`. Pairing `mb=64` + `2×` GA (`8.39M` tok/step) or `4×` GA (`16.78M` tok/step) with `HybridAC N=12`, `SBDP` (`--xla_tpu_enable_sbdp=true`), `Splash sa=1024`, Paired W1/W3 GMM (`tile_m=1024`), and `PureFusedOpt` holds peak HBM to **`25.50 GiB`** (`26.6%` of `96 GiB`) and lifts `v7x-16` HF Backend MFU to **`18.58%` Steady / `18.59%` Peak (`394,592` tok/s)** — within `0.10` pp of the Native `TpuGraphTrainer` record (`18.68%`)!
2. **Async PCIe Gen5 Full Host Offload (`mb=96` = `196,608` tok/rank, `12.58M` tok/step at `18.14%` MFU in `v7x_p11b_04`):**
   - Setting `save_last_n=0, prefetch=2` in `TpuGraphTrainer` streams layer activations asynchronously between `96 GiB` Ironwood HBM and host DRAM over PCIe Gen5 with `2`-layer lookahead prefetching, enabling **`mb=96` (`4,608` tokens/expert)** at **`53.43 GiB` HBM** and sustaining **`12,037.5` tok/s/rank (`385,200` tok/s cluster-wide = `18.14%` Steady MFU)** with virtually zero PCIe stall!
3. **`BF16` Gradient ReduceScatter + `Llama4-Scout` / `HierSC` XLA Latency-Hiding Scheduler Flags (`v5p_p8_02` -> `24.87%` MFU; `v7x_p9_02` -> `18.68%` MFU):**
   - For fine-grained sparse MoE (`39.8B` total params vs only `~2.52B` active params = **`15.8×` parameter-to-FLOP sparsity amplification**!), standard FP32 gradient `ReduceScatter` ships `159.2 GB` of gradients per step across ICI for just `17.385` GFLOP/token of active MXU work.
   - Switching `--training.mixed_precision_reduce=bfloat16` halves ICI ReduceScatter payload (`159.2 GB -> 79.6 GB`) while enabling `--xla_enable_async_all_gather=true`, `--xla_tpu_enable_ag_backward_pipelining=true`, `--xla_tpu_enable_ici_ag_pipelining=true`, and `--xla_tpu_overlap_compute_collective_tc=true`, vaulting `v5p-32` Native MFU to **`23.89%` (`v5p_p9_06`)** and `v7x-16` Native MFU to **`18.68%` Steady / `18.76%` Peak MFU (`v7x_p9_02`)**!
4. **Why `Flint` (`~18.7%` `v7x` / `~25.8%` `v5p`) Differs Structurally from `Llama-4-Scout` (`54.60%` MFU) — First-Principles Roofline Physics:**
   - `Llama-4-Scout` (`109B` total, **`17B` active**, only **`16` experts top-1**, `d_ff = 8,192`) executes **`6,144` tokens/expert** (`48 × 128 × 128` MXU tiles!) with `6.4 : 1` total-to-active ratio and **`1,558 FLOPs/byte`** arithmetic intensity (`9.6×` above `v7x`'s `162 FLOPs/byte` ridge point).
   - `RecursiveSI/flint` (`39.8B` total, **`~2.52B` active**, **`256` routed experts top-6**, tiny **`d_ff = 768`** = just `6` `128`-wide columns!) has a **`15.8 : 1` total-to-active ratio** (`2.5×` worse FSDP byte/FLOP tax!) and **`~312 FLOPs/byte`** GroupedMM arithmetic intensity (`5×` lower than Scout), plus `49` layers of `256`-expert sort/permute/unpermute routing overhead. Accounting for these hardware rooflines places `Flint`'s theoretical FSDP ceiling at `~26–28%` on `v5p` (where we hit **`25.81%` peak = `>92%` of roofline!**).
5. **Intra-Package Dual-TensorCore `StaticEP=2` Breakthrough (`11.85%` Steady / `12.24%` Peak MFU, `19.73 GiB` HBM in `v7x_p12_08`) & Monotonic `EP=2..32` Topology Law:**
   - Replacing dynamic-shape `TokenDispatcher` (`EP > 1`) AND data-dependent `torch.bincount` (`Eq(u46, 128)` Dynamo guard error) with 100% static-shape `(recv_lexp.unsqueeze(1) == arange(E)).sum(0)` + `[EP, E_local, C, D]` `all_to_all_single` + `EPCompiledGradNorm` + `SplitMeshOpt` eliminated recompilation across every `EP` degree (`2, 4, 8, 16, 32`).
   - Because each TPU `v7x` Ironwood package integrates **2 TensorCores over ultra-high-bandwidth intra-package die-to-die interconnect**, **`StaticEP=2 × FSDP=16` (`v7x_p12_08`) keeps 100% of `AllToAll` traffic intra-socket (`0` inter-chip ICI hops!)**, vaulting MFU by **`+65%`** over 2-chip `StaticEP=4` (`7.17%`, `v7x_p12_09`) and **`+76%`** over 4-chip intra-host `StaticEP=8` (`6.73%`, `v7x_p11d_04`), while cutting peak HBM to **`19.73 GiB` (`20.55%`)**!
