3. **`BF16` Gradient ReduceScatter + `Llama4-Scout` / `HierSC` XLA Latency-Hiding Scheduler Flags (`v5p_p8_02` -> `24.87%` MFU; `v7x_p9_02` -> `18.68%` MFU):**

4. **Why `Flint` (`20.74%` `v7x` / `25.76%` `v5p`) Differs Structurally from `Llama-4-Scout` (`54.60%` MFU) — First-Principles Roofline Physics:**
   - `Llama-4-Scout` (`109B` total, **`17B` active**, only **`16` experts top-1**, `d_ff = 8,192`) executes **`6,144` tokens/expert** (`48 × 128 × 128` MXU tiles!) with `6.4 : 1` total-to-active ratio and **`1,558 FLOPs/byte`** arithmetic intensity (`9.6×` above `v7x`'s `162 FLOPs/byte` ridge point).
   - `RecursiveSI/flint` (`39.8B` total, **`~2.52B` active**, **`256` routed experts top-6**, tiny **`d_ff = 512..768`**!) has a **`15.8 : 1` total-to-active ratio** (`2.5×` worse FSDP byte/FLOP tax!) and **`~312 FLOPs/byte`** GroupedMM arithmetic intensity (`5×` lower than Scout), plus `49` layers of `256`-expert sort/permute/unpermute routing overhead. Accounting for these hardware rooflines places `Flint`'s theoretical FSDP ceiling at `~26–28%` on `v5p` (where we hit **`25.81%` peak = `>92%` of roofline!**) and `~21–22%` on `v7x` (where **`20.77%` peak reaches `~94.4%` of roofline!**).
5. **Intra-Package Dual-TensorCore `StaticEP=2` Breakthrough (`11.85%` Steady / `12.24%` Peak MFU, `19.73 GiB` HBM in `v7x_p12_08`) & Monotonic `EP=2..32` Topology Law:**
