## 1. Engineering Notes & Roofline Analysis

1. **Sequence-Length Scaling (`2K` → `16K`) at Constant Tokens per Rank:**
   - Holding tokens/rank/microbatch constant (`131,072` tok/chip on `v5p` and HF `v7x-16`; `98,304` tok/TensorCore on Native `v7x-16`) keeps activation memory flat (`22.84 GiB` on `v5p`, `25.50 GiB` on HF `v7x-16`, `20.52 GiB` on Native `v7x-16` at `8K`/`16K`). Meanwhile, the global causal attention layers increase per-token compute from `17.385` (`2K`) to `18.794` (`4K`), `21.613` (`8K`), and `27.250` (`16K`) GFLOP/tok over the same `79.6 GB` `BF16` FSDP AllGather / ReduceScatter volume.
2. **Async PCIe Gen5 Host Activation Offload (`v7x_p12e_04` & `v7x_p11b_04`):**
   - Setting `save_last_n=0, prefetch=2` in `TpuGraphTrainer` with `SBDP` + `BF16-RoPE` streams layer activations asynchronously between HBM and host DRAM with 2-layer prefetching, sustaining `394,966` tok/s (`18.60%` MFU) at `mb=64` and `385,200` tok/s (`18.14%` MFU) at `mb=96`.
3. **`BF16` Gradient ReduceScatter & XLA Collective Overlap:**
   - Because `RecursiveSI/flint` has `39.8B` total parameters vs. `~2.52B` active parameters per token (`15.8 : 1` sparsity ratio), FP32 gradient `ReduceScatter` transfers `159.2 GB` per step. Switching `--training.mixed_precision_reduce=bfloat16` halves ReduceScatter volume to `79.6 GB`, while async AllGather and backward pipelining overlap FSDP communication with MXU execution.
4. **Sparsity Ratio Comparison (`Flint` vs. Coarser MoEs):**
   - Models such as `Llama-4-Scout` (`109B` total, `17B` active, `16` experts top-1, `d_ff = 8,192`) have a `6.4 : 1` total-to-active parameter ratio and large per-expert matrices. `RecursiveSI/flint` (`39.8B` total, `~2.52B` active, `256` experts top-6, `d_ff = 512`) has a `15.8 : 1` total-to-active ratio (`2.5×` higher FSDP bytes per active FLOP) and smaller expert tiles, making `BF16` ReduceScatter, `SBDP`, fused optimizer steps, and longer sequence lengths key levers for improving MFU.
5. **Static-Shape Expert Parallelism (`StaticEP=2..32`):**
   - Replacing dynamic-shape `TokenDispatcher` and `torch.bincount` with fixed-capacity `(recv_lexp.unsqueeze(1) == arange(E)).sum(0)` + `[EP, E_local, C, D]` `all_to_all_single` + `EPCompiledGradNorm` + `SplitMeshOpt` eliminates XLA recompilation across `EP = 2, 4, 8, 16, 32`. On `v7x`, `StaticEP=2 × FSDP=16` (`v7x_p12_08`) keeps `AllToAll` inside the dual-TensorCore package (`0` inter-chip hops), achieving `11.85%` steady MFU at `19.73 GiB` HBM.
